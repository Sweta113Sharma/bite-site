Read the reason on each row first. These are not all the same problem, and two of them want opposite actions.

  • CAPTURED — money taken against an order that could not be honoured. The student was charged and got nothing. Refund it from the support desk.
  • REFUND_PENDING — a refund was asked for and its outcome is not known yet. Do not refund it again: it may already have gone through. These normally settle themselves within about ten minutes, either when Razorpay's webhook confirms or when the reconciliation sweep asks. Anything still sitting here after that has been checked at least three times and wants a look at the Razorpay dashboard before anyone touches it.
