Delete your BiteSite account

You can permanently remove your identifying account details from any web browser.

Delete through My account
  1. Sign in to BiteSite with the student account you want to delete.
  2. Open My account.
  3. Under Danger Zone, choose Delete Account and confirm.

If you cannot sign in, use Forgot password first. These browser steps work even if the Android app has been uninstalled.

What deletion does

Deletion removes your name, email address, phone number, roll number, usable password, and notification tokens. Your current session is signed out immediately and the deactivated account cannot be used again.

Order and payment records are retained only for the canteen's operational and accounting obligations. They are anonymised so they no longer identify you. See the privacy policy for full details.
